Build next semester's schedule from your degree audit.

ClassPick reads your audit, finds what you still need, suggests courses you're eligible for, and lays them out with no time conflicts.

  1. Upload degree auditPDF or text from your student portal
  2. Identify remaining requirementsWhat you have left, by category
  3. Recommend eligible coursesPrerequisites checked for you
  4. Generate a conflict-free scheduleA weekly plan to bring to your advisor
Private by design

Your audit is read once on the ClassPick server to find your courses, then discarded. Nothing is saved or shared.

Advisor-friendly

ClassPick drafts a plan. Your academic advisor gives the final guidance.

Real course data

Courses, sections, and seat counts come from the UMass Boston course listings. Seat counts can change quickly, so check before you register.

Export your audit as a PDF from your student portal and add it here. It's sent securely to ClassPick, read once, and never stored.
