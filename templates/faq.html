Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about degree audit scanning, course pools, and schedule generation.

Website & General Overview
Is ClassPick an official university registration tool? ▼

No, ClassPick is an independent UMass Boston IT capstone project built by students. It acts as an course advising helper to generate conflict-free schedules. Official enrollment must be done through WISER and strongly encouraged to review all the courses generated before enrolling.

Does ClassPick store my student data or degree audit? ▼

Never, your degree audit is scanned in memory during your active browser session. No personal identifiers, grades, or transcripts are stored in our databases or saved elsewhere.

Scope Limits & Manual Elective Selection
Why doesn't ClassPick include United States or International Diversity requirements? ▼

The university's public course catalog does not include any indication of diversity attributes to the courses listed. Therefore, it would be impossible to know.

Why do I need to manually add courses for "General Education and Elective Requirements Needed"? ▼

General Education distribution areas encompass hundreds of courses and so are level-specific courses, which if we automatically added all these courses into the generation pool would overwhelm the solver. Also it would be much better if the user reviewed the course and chose the one they like rather than blindly taking an unknown course that was randomly selected.

Why are non-course milestones like MAP miles or Writing Proficiency portfolios excluded? ▼

Milestones such as the Management Achievement Program (MAP) miles, Writing Proficiency Requirement (WPR/EPR) portfolios, and minimum GPA checks are non-course degree benchmarks rather than credit-bearing classroom sections. Because they do not have weekly meeting days, times, or section codes, they cannot be placed on a schedule timetable. ClassPick tracks course scheduling, while tracking non-course milestones remains managed in WISER.

Course Prerequisites & Academic Eligibility
How does ClassPick check course prerequisites? ▼

ClassPick scans your completed courses and earned credit totals directly from your degree audit, then compares them against the university's course catalog prerequisite. If a course requires multiple prerequisites (for example, AF 210 and AF 211) and you have already completed one of them, ClassPick only warns you about the specific requirement you have not yet fulfilled (e.g., Needs AF 211) rather than repeating courses you've already passed.

Why does ClassPick ignore the phrase "or equivalent" in prerequisites? ▼

Many university course descriptions state requirements like "MATH 130 or equivalent". While your degree audit records official transfer courses and their approved UMass Boston course numbers, neither the audit nor the database catalog contains subjective rules for what other unlisted coursework, high school preparation, or AP credit might be deemed "equivalent." To prevent courses from being falsely rejected or blocked by an automated algorithm, ClassPick evaluates only explicit course codes and credit thresholds, leaving subjective equivalencies for you and your academic advisor to verify.

Why are "Permission of Instructor" or "Department Consent" not automatically verified? ▼

Permissions and overrides are manual administrative decisions made between you, your professor, and department heads—often documented via email or permission forms rather than permanent transcript codes. Because an automated scheduler cannot know if an instructor has granted you an override, ClassPick will block these courses. Use ignore prerequisites to bypass this.

What about major/degree or year-level restrictions? ▼

Administrative registration filters (such as "Open to College of Management students only" or "Junior standing") are enforced live by WISER at the moment of enrollment. Because degree audits primarily track completed course credits rather than real-time registration permissions, ClassPick focuses on academic coursework and credit minimums. Always double-check program-specific registration notes in the WISER class search.

Why is "Ignore prerequisite checks" recommended when generating schedules? ▼

Many students often have ways to receive prerequisite waivers from faculty advisors. Keeping this option checked ensures the timetable solver doesn't refuse to generate an otherwise perfect weekly schedule due to an automated mismatch. ClassPick will still display amber warning banners (⚠️ Missing Prerequisite) on any scheduled course card that needs attention so you can review it before registering.

Audit Upload & Degree Audit
Why did my PDF upload fail with a size limit error? ▼

ClassPick enforces a strict 100 KB limit because official text-based WISER audits are typically 25 KB to 60 KB. If your file is larger, ensure you downloaded the official text PDF from WISER and not a scanned photo or image of the document.

Can I plan a semester without uploading an audit? ▼

Yes! On the planner start screen, click "Build Schedule Manually (Without Degree Audit)". You can search all courses in the catalog and add them directly to your pool.

Course Search & Gen Ed Filters
How do I filter courses by General Education category? ▼

In the "Search All University Courses" box, click the "Gen Ed (0)" dropdown. Check one or more categories to instantly filter all course offerings for the selected semester.

Why does a search result say "Already Taken"? ▼

If your uploaded audit lists a course as completed or currently in progress, ClassPick warns you so you avoid accidentally retaking a fulfilled requirement. You can still add it if you intend to repeat it.

Eligible Course Pool & Requirements
Why are requirements grouped accordingly? ▼

Each requirement block represents an unfulfilled sub-requirement scanned from your audit (e.g. "Choose 1 from CS 210, CS 240"). Groups are collapsed by default to save screen space, displaying the total course and section count on the header bar.

What happens when I lock a course from a requirement group? ▼

The requirement quota automatically decrements in real-time. Once the quota reaches zero, the entire group is satisfied and safely hidden so the scheduler only looks for remaining unfulfilled slots. The section lockedd will be passed onto the "Locked-In Section".

Locked-In Section Management
What does locking a section do? ▼

Locking anchors that exact meeting time and section number into your schedule. The solver treats locked sections as non-negotiable fixed blocks and calculates remaining courses around them without time collisions.

Why did I receive a "Time Conflict Detected" alert when locking? ▼

ClassPick checks real-time meeting intervals. You cannot lock two sections that overlap on the same day and time. Unlock or edit the conflicting section first to proceed.

Custom & Unlisted Courses
When should I use "Add Custom / Unlisted Section"? ▼

Use this if you are enrolled in an independent study, work study, off-campus internship, or unlisted courses that isn't yet present in our database. It blocks out the time on your weekly timetable so no other classes collide with it. Later you will have to manually go into WISER and sign up for that specific course that you've found.

Restrictions & Time Filters
How do the earliest start and latest end time filters work? ▼

The solver will reject any section starting before your earliest start time or running past your latest end time. Asynchronous online courses without fixed meeting hours are unaffected unless you check "Exclude online courses".

What should I do if "No valid schedule combinations found" appears? ▼

This indicates your restrictions were too tight to fit your target course load. Try checking additional allowed meeting days, broadening your time window, or checking "Ignore course capacity limits".

Class Codes & WISER Registration
How do I use the 4 or 5 digit class code in WISER? ▼

In your generated schedule list, click the [Copy] button next to the class number (e.g. #4802). In WISER, go to Enrollment: Add Classes, paste the number into the Enter Class Number box, and submit.
