Build next semester's schedule from your degree audit.

ClassPick scans your official UMass Boston degree audit, calculates what you still need, checks course eligibility, and generates conflict-free schedules in seconds.

Advisor-friendly

Instant Audit Scanning

Fast WISER Enrollment

Dramatic Time Saver
