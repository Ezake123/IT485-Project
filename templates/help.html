Help and Preparation Guide

Follow this 2-step chronological guide to download your official degree audit from WISER and register using your generated class codes.

Phase 1: Pre-Planning

How to Download Your Degree Audit

  1. Log into WISER at umb.edu/wiser.
  2. Find and click Boston Degree Audit on the home dashboard.
  3. Click Run Current Program without altering options.
  4. Wait for the report to generate. In the audit list, open the newest row and select View PDF.
  5. Click Download PDF Audit and save it to your device (audits are typically under 60 KB).
Phase 2: Uploading Audit & Schedule Generation

Uploading Audit/Building Schedule Manually

  1. Inside the schedule builder page, you can either drag the audit that you've downloaded into the box or click the box and choose the audit. Skip this if you are building schedule manually.
  2. Select the Semester you want to generate for by choosing the Semester Season and Year. Select this also if you are building it manually.
  3. Click Scan Degree Audit and move onto the next part of phase 2.
  4. If you are manually building the schedule, click Build Schedule Manually and proceed onto the next part of phase 2.

How to Generate Schedule

  1. Check if there is any requirements inside General Education and Elective Requirements Needed.
  2. Go down to Search All University Courses and find a course you would like to take for that requirement
  3. Take a look inside Eligible Generator Course Pool and adjust anything to your perferences.
  4. Go down to Schedule Restrictions & Constraints and also adjust it to your preferences.
  5. Finally click Solve & Generate Conflict-Free Schedule once you are satisfy with everything.
Phase 3: Post-Planning & Enrollment

How to Register Using Class Codes in WISER

  1. In your ClassPick generated schedule, click the [Copy] button next to any course to copy its class code (e.g. #4802).
  2. Log into WISER at umb.edu/wiser.
  3. Open the Enrollment dropdown on the left navigation and select Enrollment: Add Classes.
  4. Select your target semester term.
  5. Paste your copied class code into the Enter Class Number box and submit to add it to your shopping cart.
